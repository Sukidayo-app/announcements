Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

With this update, you can now **record memories together** when you complete something on your wish list! Add photos and a short note to look back on your special moments together✨

---

## 🎊 Complete a wish, then save a memory!

When you check off a wish, a celebration screen will appear. Tap **"Save a Memory"** to open the memory registration form.

**What you can add**

- 📷 Photos (Free users: 1 photo / Riamo Plus: up to 3 photos)
- 💬 A short note (up to 500 characters)
- 📅 Date of completion

---

## 📋 Look back on everything you've accomplished

In the "Completed" tab of your wish list, you can view all your completed wishes and the memories you've saved anytime.
