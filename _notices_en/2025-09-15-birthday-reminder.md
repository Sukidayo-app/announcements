Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

Starting with version 1.6.0, we've added several new features we'd love to tell you about!

---

## 🎂 Birthday Reminder is here!

Register your birthdays and Riamo will send you a reminder notification as your partner's birthday approaches — so you never miss that special day!

**How to set your birthday:**

1. Open the ⚙️ Menu screen
2. Tap "Basic Settings"
3. Tap "Date of Birth" under personal info
4. Enter your date of birth and tap "Save"

---

## 🛠️ Wish List categories are now customizable!

You can now customize the categories in your Wish List! Create categories that match your lifestyle and interests as a couple.

**How to edit categories:**

1. Open your Wish List
2. Tap "···" in the top left
3. Tap "✏️ Edit Categories"
4. Freely add or edit categories

> You can add up to 10 categories.

---

## 👍 Send feedback on questions!

You can now send feedback on questions from the results screen after both of you have answered. Let us know which questions you loved — or found tricky — to help us improve future content!
