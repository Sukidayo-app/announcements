## About Ads and Subscription

Starting in mid-February 2026, an ad will be displayed after you answer your daily question.

We know this isn't what everyone wants to see, and it wasn't an easy decision. But this change is necessary to keep Riamo running and improving for the long term, and we hope you'll understand.

Alongside this, we're also launching **Riamo Plus**, our subscription plan.

Because Riamo is built for couples, subscribing covers both partners — if one of you subscribes, you both get access to Riamo Plus. The core features of Riamo will remain free, so please don't worry!

- ✅ No ads
- ✅ Access to all Couple Quiz themes
