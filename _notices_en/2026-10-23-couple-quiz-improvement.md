Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

Thank you as always for using Riamo.

We've heard from many of you: "I wish I could save my progress…" and "I want to fix my answer after submitting!" **Good news: you can already do both** 🙌

These features might have slipped under the radar, so here's a quick guide ✨

---
<br>  

## ✏️ Edit your answer until your partner answers

Changed your mind, or made a typo? No problem!<br>
**As long as your partner hasn't answered yet, you can change your answer.**

1. On the waiting screen, tap the **✏️ (pencil) icon** next to your answer
2. Tap the answer you want and select "**Change**"
3. When you see "Answer updated," you're all set 🎉

> Once your partner has answered, editing is no longer available so that you can both see the results fairly.

<img width="100%" alt="Edit answer" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/notice/couple-quiz-improvement/edit_en.png" />

---
<br>  

## 💾 Answer part of a quiz and pick up where you left off

No need to answer every question in one go!<br>
**If you close the screen partway through, your answers so far are saved automatically.**

- Tap ✕ while answering and you'll see "Stop for now?" Choose "**Stop (saved)**" to keep your answers
- To continue, go to "**Quizzes in progress**" in the Couple Quizzes list and tap "**Resume**"
- You can see at a glance how many questions you've answered 👀

> Your in-progress answers are saved on your device, so you can't continue on a different device.

<img width="100%" alt="Save and resume" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/notice/couple-quiz-improvement/save_en.png" />

---
<br>  

Answer a little at a time in your spare moments, or take a moment to rethink and update your answer.<br>
Enjoy Couple Quizzes at your own pace 💗
