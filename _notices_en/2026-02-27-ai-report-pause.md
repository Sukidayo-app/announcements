date: 2026-02-27

Hi, this is the Riamo team.

Thank you for using Riamo.

We'd like to inform you that the **AI Report** feature will be temporarily paused in early March.

AI Report was created with the hope of helping couples reflect on their relationship more deeply. However, we've determined that the quality — both in accuracy and overall experience — hasn't yet reached the standard we're aiming for. Rather than continuing with something half-finished, we'd like to take the time to rethink what a truly meaningful experience looks like for the two of you.

---

## What happens next

Reports you've already received will still be viewable during the pause.

We'd love to hear your thoughts — please share any feedback or ideas with us:

[Riamo AI Report Feedback Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeOrQWEG2LX3yECrxJqdMuNqPut3Xnp2kyYi4zTQL0DdJo9oQ/viewform)

We're committed to finding a better way to support your relationship.

Thank you for your understanding.

The Riamo Team
