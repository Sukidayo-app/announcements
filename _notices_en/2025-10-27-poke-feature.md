Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

Starting with version 1.7.1, we've added the **Poke feature** — a fun way to send your partner a gentle nudge to answer their question! 🎉

Through your animals, you can give your partner a little "poke 👈" to remind them to answer — perfect for staying connected even on busy days ✨

---

## How to use Poke

1. Tap the speech bubble icon "👈" on your partner's animal
2. Choose a poke that matches your mood
3. Your partner receives a notification!

> You can only send a poke after you've answered and while waiting for your partner's response.
> You can send up to one poke per hour.
