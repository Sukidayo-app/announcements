Hello! This is the Riamo team.

We've just released a new update for Riamo. This update includes a major redesign of the Anniversary feature, along with two new features: Message Cards and My Page 🎉

Here's a look at what's new!

---

## 🎉 Anniversary Feature Power-Up!

You can now register **unlimited** anniversaries — no more 3-item limit! Add as many special dates as you like, from your dating anniversary and birthdays to your first date or the day you met.

Each anniversary now has its own detail screen, where you can check milestones like 100 days, 200 days, and 1,000 days together in your "Anniversary Journey." You can also set countdown repeat options (yearly, monthly, or no repeat) and enable reminder notifications as the date approaches.

**How to add an anniversary:**

1. Go to My Page
2. Tap ">" in the Anniversary section to open the anniversary list
3. Tap the "+" button at the bottom right
4. Enter the name, date, icon, and countdown settings, then tap "Add"

> Your partner's birthdays, your dating anniversary, and the date you started Riamo are default anniversaries and cannot be deleted. Anniversaries set in the previous version will carry over, but if they overlap with default anniversaries, please delete the duplicates manually.

---

## 💌 Message Cards Are Here!

You can now send message cards to your partner 💌 Choose a card design and share your everyday appreciation or make a special anniversary moment even more meaningful.

The day before an anniversary, a pop-up in the app will prompt you to write a message card — a perfect reminder for those important days. You can also use the scheduled send feature to prepare your card in advance so it arrives right on the day.

**How to send a message card:**

1. Go to My Page
2. Tap ">" in the Message Cards section to open the card list
3. Tap the "+" button at the bottom right
4. Choose a card design and write your message (you can also edit the name)
5. Select "Send Now" or "Send on a Specific Date" and send!

> The scheduled send time is based on the recipient's timezone (e.g., if you set 12:00, the card will arrive at 12:00 local time for your partner).

---

## 👤 My Page Is Now Live!

A brand new "My Page" has been added! From My Page, you can see a count-up display of your dating anniversary, check all your registered anniversaries, and view message cards received from your partner.

The menu screen (gear icon) previously shown in the bottom navigation bar can now be accessed via the icon in the top right of My Page.
