Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

Starting with version 1.7.0, we've added the **Anniversary Celebration** feature — register your anniversaries and receive a special celebration message on the day itself! 🎉

We hope this helps you cherish every special day together!

---

## How to set up an anniversary

1. Open the ⚙️ Menu screen
2. Tap any item under "Anniversary" at the top
3. Enter the anniversary name and date
4. Tap "Save"

You'll receive a celebration message from the app on the day! 🎉

> You can register up to 3 anniversaries.
