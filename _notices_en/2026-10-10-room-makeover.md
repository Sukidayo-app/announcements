Hi there! This is the Riamo team.

Thank you as always for using Riamo.

In our last notice about the design refresh, we hinted, "And maybe one day you'll be able to decorate the room, too…? 👀"

**Thanks for waiting! With the next update, you'll be able to redecorate your room together** 🎉

On top of that, we're opening "**Riamo Points 💎**," which you earn together, and "**Beaver's Furniture Shop 🦫**," where you can buy furniture with your points!

Here's a sneak peek at what you'll be able to do ✨

---
<br>  

## 🏠 Redecorate your room

You'll be able to redecorate the room your animals live in and make it **a room that's just for the two of you**!

Not only can you place furniture wherever you like, you can also **change the floor and wallpaper** 🎨<br>
Put a little something on the table, set a houseplant next to the sofa… fill the room with everything you two love.

### 🎁 Some furniture comes with a fun little surprise…?

Some pieces of furniture do a little more than just sit there 🎶

For example, try moving the window to a different spot…? 🪟

Decorate your room and discover what other surprises are waiting for you.

### 👫 Your room is shared by the two of you

Your room is shared. When one of you redecorates, the change shows up in your partner's room too.<br>
"Look what I did!" — you can build your room together, little by little 🏡

---
<br>  

## 💎 Start earning Riamo Points

**Check the results of your daily questions or a Couple Quiz together, or add your memory of the month, and you'll earn "Riamo Points"!**

Points are shared by you two. You earn them together and spend them together 💞

> Couples with Riamo Plus earn **2x** Riamo Points ✨

---
<br>  

## 🦫 Beaver's Furniture Shop is now open!

Take the points you've saved and head to **Beaver's Furniture Shop**!<br>
Along with furniture like sofas and beds, you'll find floors and wallpaper too 🛋️

- 🎨 **Choose a color**: Some furniture lets you pick the color you like
- 👀 **Try it in your room**: Place it in your room before buying to see how it looks
- ✏️ **Request furniture**: Tell the shop, "We'd love furniture like this!"

### 💬 "Can I buy this?" Ask your partner

The points belong to both of you, so you'll want to choose furniture together, too.<br>
When you find something you like, tap "Ask your partner" to **check with your partner**.<br>
Your partner can reply with a message like "Sure!", so enjoy talking it over and building your room together 💌

---
<br>  

## 🪑 About your current furniture

Until now, furniture arrived as you answered the daily questions. Starting with this update, it changes to a system where you can **choose and buy the furniture you like with Riamo Points**.

> All the furniture you've received so far can still be used after the update. Furniture you already have will switch to its new design.
