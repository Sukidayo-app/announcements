Hello from the Riamo team!

Thank you for using Riamo as always.

In this update, we've added a new **"Refer a friend"** feature 🎉
When you refer a friend couple, **both the friend couple and the two of you get 1 couple quiz ticket each!**

---
<br>

## 🎁 Get a quiz ticket by referring a friend

Just send your couple's Referral Code to a friend.

1. From the menu, tap **"Refer a friend"**
2. Tap **"Refer a friend"** to send a message with your Referral Code (you can also use **"Copy message"**)
3. When your friend starts Riamo, they enter the code at **"Do you have a Referral Code?"**
4. Once your friend pairs up with their partner, **both the friend couple and the two of you get 1 ticket each** 🎫

**For friends who received a code**
Even after pairing up, they can enter it anytime from the menu via **"Refer a friend" → "Enter Referral Code"** (within 30 days of pairing, once per couple).

> ・Entering the code alone is not enough. Tickets arrive when your friend pairs up with their partner.
> ・You can earn tickets for up to 5 couples per month. Your friends always get theirs.
> ・People already using Riamo and your own Referral Code are not eligible.

## 📷 You can now send photos in comments

A much-requested feature! Tap the **📷 button** in the comment field to attach and send photos.
Up to 2 photos per comment, and you can send a photo on its own too.

---
<br>

## 💌 Choose a photo for your message card cover

Message card covers can now use **a photo of the two of you**, not just illustrations.
Tap **"Use a photo"** when creating a card, and you can adjust the position and cropping.
