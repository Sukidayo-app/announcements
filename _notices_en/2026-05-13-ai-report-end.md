Hi, this is the Riamo team.

Thank you for using Riamo.

After careful consideration, we've decided to **discontinue the AI Report feature at the end of May**. The feature has been paused since March, and we've concluded that the best path forward is to end the current version entirely.

---

> ⚠️ **Viewing and saving your past AI Reports**
>
> As of **May 31, 2026 (Sun) 23:59**, all previously received AI Reports will no longer be accessible.
>
> If you'd like to keep any of your reports, please save them via screenshot before the deadline.

---

## How we got here

AI Report was designed to help couples gain deeper insights into their relationship by analyzing responses to daily questions with AI.

We received wonderful feedback like "The analysis was spot on and fun!" but also heard requests for "more new discoveries and deeper analysis."

On our end, we also felt that the quality of the analysis and the naturalness of the writing hadn't reached the level we wanted to deliver.

After the pause, we continued exploring ways to improve, but ultimately decided it would be better to end the feature in its current form and rethink it from the ground up, rather than resume with half-finished quality.

## What's next

We'll be taking everything we've learned from your feedback to reconsider what truly meaningful analysis and experiences look like for couples — and work toward bringing something better to you in the future.

We sincerely apologize to those who were looking forward to the return of AI Reports.

Our team is committed to building features that support your relationship, and we hope you'll continue to enjoy Riamo.

The Riamo Team
